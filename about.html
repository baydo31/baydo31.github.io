About Me

My name is Diane Lapindo, born on May 31, 2007. I am currently a criminology student at the University of the Cordilleras, where I am working toward achieving my academic and personal goals.

I come from Pula Poblacion, Tuba, Benguet, a place that has shaped who I am today and continues to inspire me to strive for a better future. Growing up in a close-knit community has taught me the values of hard work, perseverance, and staying grounded.

As a criminology student, I am passionate about understanding the law, maintaining peace and order, and contributing to the safety of society. I am dedicated to learning and improving myself every day, both academically and personally. I aim to balance my studies with personal growth as I prepare for the responsibilities and challenges of my chosen profession.

My dream is to become a member of the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP), where I can serve the community, help save lives, and protect people and properties from fire and other emergencies. I am determined to work hard and stay committed to reaching this goal.

I believe that education is a powerful tool that will help me achieve my dreams and make a positive impact in my community, especially in promoting safety, service, and public welfare.
